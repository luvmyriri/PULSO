- `NativeGuestReportSheet` reuses `NativeBottomSheet`, native theme variables, a 48 px primary/secondary action pair, and the existing modal close control. The extra receipt reminder was removed at the user's subsequent request. English and Filipino copy have the same meaning.
- `NativeBottomSheet` focuses its title on opening, confines Tab/Shift+Tab to visible enabled controls, and restores focus to the trigger when dismissed. Escape, Android Back, the close button, backdrop, and the existing swipe handle dismiss the sheet. Its body scrolls at short heights; native safe-area padding remains active. Themes preserve actions and order; focus uses 3 px and forced-colors controls remain visible.
- `ResidentDraftStatus` hides the initial new-draft notice. Storage errors, conflicts, restored/expired drafts and uncertain-submission recovery remain available. `WizardCardFooter` hides the routine unsubmitted note in native Android; the final review guidance remains.
- `LocationAccuracyBadge` is shared by full and Quick Report. A device fix uses its actual positive `deviceFix.accuracy_m`, including estimates at or below 25 m, to display `Approx. ±Xm`. Unknown accuracy has no invented number. Manual/search points retain unknown precision; a display-only map center has no acquired-location badge. The submitted provenance is unchanged.
- The native camera step removes the Change location link. If location is unavailable, denied, outside jurisdiction, or has an acquisition error, the existing location editor is shown for recovery. Browser reporting retains its existing location editor control.
- `LocationAddressPanel` removes the address-details-unavailable message at the user's request. It retains available address/Plus Code/coordinates without claiming successful geocoding; genuine empty-location and loading states remain distinct.
