These prompts produced visual planning boards for the complete government website. The boards establish composition and interface character. The companion specification governs behavior, permissions, lifecycle, copy, states, accessibility, responsive behavior, and data truth.

## Shared generation constraints

Every generation was constrained to:

- a professional government operations workspace, not a public marketing dashboard;
- quiet editorial hierarchy, compact persistent navigation, strong route and record context, and restrained surfaces;
- Morning Field Sheet light mode with mineral canvas, white work surfaces, crisp rules, restrained shadows, and Field Blue actions;
- Civic Aperture evidence or location emphasis, Signal Cut lifecycle language, Coordinate Rail metadata, and local engineering context;
- minimal color and familiar icons, with no glassmorphism, neon glow, gradient spectacle, floating decorative cards, aurora effects, particles, or science-fiction command-center styling;
- small but readable typography, compact information density, generous negative space, precise alignment, accessible contrast, and visible focus treatment;
- realistic but clearly `SAMPLE` civic records only; and
- Figma-style multi-frame presentation at high fidelity, with complete page hierarchy and reusable patterns.

## Board 1 — Work orders and projects

**Output:** `government-work-delivery-family-v1.0.png`

**Prompt intent:** Create one cohesive desktop interface board showing four complete PULSO government views: a role-aware work-order queue, a selected work-order engineering docket, a projects portfolio/list, and a project docket with a governed approval decision. Preserve filters, saved views, freshness, assignee/team, priority, lifecycle, evidence/location, milestones, dependencies, cost/progress context, immutable activity, and consequence-aware actions. The result should be robust and operational while visually minimal.

## Board 2 — Technical, inspection, procurement, and cost work

**Output:** `government-technical-compliance-family-v1.0.png`

**Prompt intent:** Create a cohesive desktop board for OBO inspections, procurement requests, design and survey work, and Bill of Quantities/estimation. Use engineering-document composition, compact ruled tables, quiet property groups, document/evidence apertures, clear compliance outcomes, lineage, revision context, totals, approval gates, and accessible next actions. Avoid decorative engineering graphics and show technical detail only when it represents usable data.

## Board 3 — Records, insights, and administration

**Output:** `government-records-insights-admin-family-v1.0.png`

**Prompt intent:** Create a cohesive desktop board for documents and records, timeline and audit, operational insights/predictive review, and administration. Records must show classification, retention, version, source, and governed archive controls. Audit must read as immutable history with filters and actor/source context. Insights must show metric definition, source, freshness, confidence/limitations, summaries, and table access—not a KPI wall. Administration must show people/roles, resident-account governance, settings, and agent access with explicit scope and authorization.

## Board 4 — Interaction system

**Output:** `government-interaction-system-v1.0.png`

**Prompt intent:** Design the complete PULSO government interaction system on one Figma-style board. Include success, information, warning, recoverable error, blocking error, progress, and undo toasts; offline, stale, partial-data, permission, emergency-access, and maintenance banners; a notification center with read/unread, priority, source, timestamp, deep-link context, loading, empty, failure, and pagination; governed dialogs, drawers, popovers, sheets, confirmation patterns, reason capture, impact summary, typed confirmation for high-risk actions, and focus-return notes; scoped command search; skeleton, first-use empty, filtered empty, offline, conflict, permission-limited, provider failure, system failure, no-eligible-team, unsaved-recovery, and success states. Keep every surface calm, compact, legible, and traceable.

## Board 5 — End-to-end process blueprints

**Output:** `government-process-blueprints-v1.0.png`

**Prompt intent:** Create four detailed but visually restrained government service-blueprint lanes: citizen-report triage and assignment; work-order field execution and completion; project, procurement, cost, and OBO inspection; and records, communications, permissions, and emergency governance. For every stage show responsible role, authoritative record, required evidence, allowed state transition, notification, failure/recovery path, and persisted audit event. Use a precise horizontal workflow with restrained color and Signal Cut continuity rather than decorative flowchart styling.

## Usage and limitations

- Do not derive lifecycle, permission, status, record, or API truth from pixels in these boards.
- Do not use generated people, coordinates, costs, ticket IDs, timestamps, predictions, evidence, or outcomes as production data.
- Recreate approved patterns as semantic variables, Auto Layout components, variants, and linked instances in the existing PULSO Figma file.
- Validate every Figma reconstruction structurally and visually; a raster board alone is not a design-system component or implementation handoff.
- Maintain identical meaning, control boundaries, target geometry, and recovery paths in light and dark modes.
