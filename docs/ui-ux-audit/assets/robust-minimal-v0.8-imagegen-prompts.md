## Resident family

**Primary request:** Create one high-fidelity PULSO Resident design board with four connected surfaces: public landing, dedicated resident sign-in, authenticated home, and report detail/tracking. The product is a civic infrastructure reporting service for North Caloocan. It must feel minimalist, aesthetic, professional, selectively cinematic, and feature-complete.

**Interaction direction:** Use Notion-like principles only: calm persistent navigation, page hierarchy, database-like lists, properties, saved context, progressive disclosure, search, and confident density. Do not copy Notion trade dress.

**Required content:**

- public thesis `Make the issue visible.`;
- `Report an issue`, `Track a report`, and `Resident sign in`;
- no government or staff access link;
- one true-color local civic aperture with sample provenance;
- how it works, updates, services/emergency, privacy/language;
- dedicated resident fields, recovery, registration, language, and privacy reassurance;
- authenticated Home, My reports, Report, Updates, Account;
- active `PUL-2026-0822` / `Flooded street` / `In progress` object;
- latest update, next step, city updates, nearby issues, and services;
- report Signal Cut, evidence/provenance, location/list alternative, public-safe history, and state-permitted action.

**Visual system:** Morning Field Sheet, mineral canvas, white opaque surfaces, survey-blue ink, restrained rules, resident ember action, true semantic status colors, Archivo-like civic titles, Source Sans 3-like UI, IBM Plex Mono-like real record data, asymmetric editorial grids, open ruled lists, selective 8–12px containment, and one Field Aperture per viewport.

**Avoid:** glass, transparency, blur, neon, glow, gradients, black sci-fi dashboard, 3D city, fake telemetry, particles, grid texture, giant logo, giant title, card grid, icon overload, decorative KPI, generic stepper, social engagement patterns, tiny type, watermark, fabricated verification.
