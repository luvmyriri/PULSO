Problem Statement

SOP: The Urban Friction

Visibility Gap

LGUs lack a centralized, real-time command center for infrastructure health monitoring.

Report Redundancy

DBSCAN groups duplicate complaints, solving the bottleneck of manual complaint sorting.

Triage Inefficiency

High-priority hazards are often buried under low-priority reports without AI categorization.

Social Governance

Reliance on Facebook/social media creates unstructured "noise" instead of actionable data.

Solution Scope

Project Scope & Goals

A full-stack ecosystem bridging citizens and government engineers.

Citizen Mobility

Snap & Pin feature with automated GPS metadata and offline sync capabilities.

Gemini Vision AI

Multimodal classification (Images/Text) with automated Severity Scoring (1-10).

Dispatcher GIS

Live, color-coded Heat Maps visualizing red zones for rapid deployment.

Field Crew Loop

Digital job tickets with "Proof of Repair" validation for project closure.

Constraints

System Limitations

Adoption Rate

Heat maps only reach maximum fidelity with significant citizen participation.

Signal Dependency

Real-time sync requires cellular data; deep urban canyons may delay GPS precision.

Property Boundary

The system is strictly localized to public infrastructure, excluding private properties.

Technical Pillar

Methodology: Waterfall Model

Ensuring compliance with RA 10173 and RA 9184 legislative frameworks.

01. Design

Modeling spatial logic via Haversine and DBSCAN algorithms.

02. Development

Asynchronous operations using Python FastAPI and Flutter clients.

03. Resilience

90-Day Roadmap transitioning from Digitization to Predictive Resilience.

04. Maintenance

Model Drift Mitigation through continuous feedback from verified field data.
