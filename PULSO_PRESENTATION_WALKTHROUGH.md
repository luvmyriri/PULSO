# PULSO: Presentation Walkthrough & Script
**Public Utility Locator System for Operations**
*Bridging the Gap Between Citizens and Governance through Digital Excellence.*

---

## 🎙️ Slide 1: The Vision (Introduction)
**Visual:** High-fidelity PULSO Logo, Dark "Architecture Noir" Aesthetic.
**Speaker Script:**
"Good day. We are Team PULSO. Today, we are presenting a paradigm shift in how cities breathe and operate. PULSO is not just an app; it is a specialized Software-as-a-Service ecosystem designed to transition Local Government Units from reactive crisis management to proactive, predictive urban resilience."

---

## 🚨 Slide 2: The Problem (The Visibility Gap)
**Visual:** A graphic showing fragmented communication (Social Media noise, paper logs, phone hotlines).
**Speaker Script:**
"Local governance currently suffers from a 'Visibility Gap.' Infrastructure failures are often reported through informal channels like Facebook, which lack geospatial precision. This leads to:
1. **The Silo Effect:** Data is scattered and unanalyzable.
2. **Report Redundancy:** Hundreds of calls for a single broken transformer.
3. **Triage Inefficiency:** Critical hazards getting buried under low-priority noise."

---

## 🛡️ Slide 3: The Solution (PULSO Defined)
**Visual:** A clean diagram showing the three-tier ecosystem (Citizen App, AI Backend, LGU Command Center).
**Speaker Script:**
"PULSO solves this by empowering every resident to act as a 'human sensor.' We integrate **Gemini AI** and **Geospatial Analytics** to sanitize, cluster, and prioritize reports in real-time. We turn raw complaints into actionable, professional-grade work orders."

---

## 🛠️ Slide 4: Core Technical Modules (The Engine)
**Visual:** 4 key icons: Snap & Pin, Surgical Triage, GDM, and Heat Map.
**Speaker Script:**
"Our technology stack is built on four core pillars:
1. **Snap & Pin:** Automated GPS metadata extraction—eliminating location ambiguity.
2. **Gemini AI Surgical Triage:** Using Vision-Language Models to categorize issues and assign a 'Severity Score' (1-10) within seconds.
3. **Geospatial Deduplication (GDM):** Utilizing the DBSCAN algorithm to merge duplicate reports into a single 'Incident Cluster.'
4. **Operational Heat Map:** A live, color-coded GIS interface for LGU decision-makers."

---

## 💻 Slide 5: The Technical Blueprint
**Visual:** Logos of Flutter, React, FastAPI, MySQL, and Google Gemini.
**Speaker Script:**
"PULSO is engineered for high-concurrency environments:
- **Frontend:** Flutter for the mobile client; React + Vite for the heavy-duty Command Center.
- **Backend:** Python FastAPI for asynchronous orchestration.
- **Database:** MySQL with Spatial Extensions (SRID 4326) for precise planetary mathematics."

---

## 🔄 Slide 6: The Workflow (Step-by-Step)
**Visual:** A carousel or timeline of the report lifecycle.
**Speaker Script:**
"This is the PULSO journey:
- **Phase 1:** A citizen submits a report.
- **Phase 2:** Gemini AI validates the image and categorizes the hazard.
- **Phase 3:** GDM clusters it with existing reports to avoid redundancy.
- **Phase 4:** The LGU Dispatcher views the 'Red Zone' on the map and assigns a crew.
- **Phase 5:** The 'Automated Status Loop' notifies the citizen at every stage, restoring public trust."

---

## 📊 Slide 7: Methodology & Compliance
**Visual:** Waterfall Model diagram and RA 10173/RA 9184 logos.
**Speaker Script:**
"We employ a strict **Waterfall Model** to ensure compliance with Philippine law. We are 100% aligned with the **Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)** and the **Government Procurement Reform Act (RA 9184)**, ensuring every 'Program of Works' is transparent and audit-ready."

---

## 🚀 Slide 8: The 90-Day Roadmap (Vision)
**Visual:** A 3-phase timeline: Digitization → Prevention → Predictive.
**Speaker Script:**
"Our roadmap culminations in **Predictive Mastery.** By analyzing historical failure rates (MTBF) and image-based degradation, PULSO will eventually predict where a transformer or bridge might fail *before* it happens. We are moving Caloocan North toward a future of predictive urban resilience."

---

## 🏁 Slide 9: Conclusion
**Visual:** Contact details and "Total Orange" branding.
**Speaker Script:**
"PULSO is the heartbeat of a smarter city. We invite you to join us in bridging the gap between citizens and governance. Thank you."
